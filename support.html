Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a book?

Go to the Library tab and tap import. You can import text files (TXT, EPUB) or paste text directly. Lexima will build your personal corpus from the content.

How do I import a video with subtitles?

Lexima supports three ways to get subtitles for your videos:

  • Video with embedded subtitles — Import the video file directly. Lexima detects and extracts subtitles already muxed into the file.
  • Video + separate subtitle file — Import your video first, then import a subtitle file (SRT, VTT, ASS) to pair with it.
  • Video only (on-device transcription) — Import just the video. Lexima uses on-device speech-to-text to generate a transcript automatically. No internet required, no data leaves your device.

How does the flashcard system work?

As you read or watch content, Lexima tracks words you look up or mark. These are scheduled for review using a spaced repetition algorithm. Flashcards automatically pull example sentences from your own imported materials, so you review words in context you've already encountered.

How do I set up an LLM provider?

Go to Settings → LLM Integration. Enter your API key for a supported provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.). Your key is stored in your device's Keychain and never leaves your device except to communicate directly with the provider you chose.

What data does Lexima send to the LLM?

Only the text you explicitly choose to process (e.g., asking for a translation, definition, or explanation) is sent to the LLM provider you configured. Your full library and study history stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does Lexima sync across devices?

Yes. Lexima uses iCloud to sync your study progress (flashcard reviews, word mastery levels, learning streaks) across your devices signed into the same Apple ID. Your imported books, videos, and corpus data are stored locally and not uploaded to iCloud — only your progress travels with you.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Data Management → Reset Study Progress. This clears your review history and word mastery data (synced across iCloud). Your imported books and videos remain untouched. To delete everything including your library, choose "Delete All Data" instead.

On-device transcription isn't working well. What can I do?

On-device transcription uses Apple's Speech framework and depends on audio clarity and the language model installed on your device. For best results: ensure the video audio is clear, download the offline dictation language pack in iOS/macOS Settings → Keyboard → Dictation, and make sure the video's spoken language matches your device language settings. If results are still poor, try importing a separate subtitle file instead.

The app crashed / I found a bug. What do I do?

Please email us (address below) with: (1) your device model and OS version, (2) what you were doing when it happened, (3) if possible, steps to reproduce. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.
