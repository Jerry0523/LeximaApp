Privacy Policy

App: Layers

Developer: Jerry

Effective date: 2026-10-27

Overview

Layers runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers under our control.

Data Storage

Your projects, imported media, and edits are stored locally on your Mac as .layerproj documents. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

API Keys

If you choose to configure a third-party LLM service, your API keys are stored exclusively in your device's Keychain. They are never transmitted to us or any server we control.

Third-Party LLM Integration (Optional)

Layers allows you to optionally connect your own third-party LLM provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or others) using your own API key.

  • When you use this feature, content you choose to process (such as text from documents, videos, or study materials) is sent directly from your device to the LLM provider you selected.
  • We do not intercept, store, or have access to this data or your API key.
  • The third-party provider's handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review it before use.
  • You can disconnect or remove your API key at any time.

Third-Party SDKs

Layers does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Layers does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here.
