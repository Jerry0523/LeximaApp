Privacy Policy

App: Lexima  ·  Developer: Jerry  ·  Effective date: October 27, 2026

Overview

Lexima is a language learning app that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers under our control.

Data Storage

Your learning data — including books you read, videos you watch, vocabulary lists, study progress, and app settings — are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export it.

Third-Party LLM Integration (Optional)

Lexima allows you to optionally configure a third-party Large Language Model (LLM) provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or others) using your own API key. This feature enables AI-assisted learning capabilities within the app.

  • When you use this feature, content you choose to process (such as text from books, video transcripts, vocabulary items, or study notes) is sent directly from your device to the LLM provider you selected.
  • Your API key is stored exclusively in your device's Keychain. It is never transmitted to us or any server we control.
  • We do not intercept, log, store, or have access to the data you send to third-party LLM providers.
  • The third-party provider's handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review it before use.
  • You can disconnect or remove your API key at any time in app settings.

API Keys

Any API keys you provide (for LLM services or other integrations) are stored securely in your device's system Keychain. They are never included in app backups sent to iCloud (unless you explicitly enable Keychain sync in your system settings), never shared with us, and never transmitted anywhere except directly to the service you configured.

Third-Party SDKs

Lexima does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that collect data. The app contains no tracking code of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Lexima does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this URL.
